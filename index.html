Risk Classification

5-Level Risk System.

Every position is scored in real time based on health factor proximity to the liquidation threshold.

Critical
HF < 1.05. Liquidation imminent. Cascade alert active.
Danger
HF 1.05–1.10. High probability. May trigger within hours.
Warning
HF 1.10–1.20. Significant moves could trigger liquidation.
Caution
HF 1.20–1.50. Moderate risk in volatile conditions.
Safe
HF > 1.50. Well-collateralized, comfortable buffer.

  positions(
    where: { riskLevel: "CRITICAL" }
    orderBy: healthFactor
    orderDirection: asc
    first: 20

    id
    user
    healthFactor
    totalCollateralUSD
    totalDebtUSD
    riskLevel
    lastUpdatedBlock
  }
