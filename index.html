What Problem Does This Solve?

You do not need to be a DeFi expert to understand the core idea: this tool tracks where borrowing positions are becoming dangerous.

Simple explanation

DeFi liquidations can move markets fast.

In DeFi lending platforms such as Aave or Compound, users can borrow assets by depositing collateral. For example, someone may deposit ETH and borrow stablecoins.

If the value of their collateral falls too much, the protocol can automatically liquidate the position. That means the collateral is sold to repay the debt.

When many positions are close to liquidation at the same time, a price movement can trigger a chain reaction. DeFi Risk Monitor is designed to detect those vulnerable areas before they become a bigger problem.

This is not financial advice. The tool provides risk data and monitoring, but it does not tell users what to buy or sell.
1

People borrow using collateral

A user deposits crypto, such as ETH, and borrows another asset. The deposited crypto protects the lender.

2

The collateral value can fall

If the market drops, the value of the user’s collateral may no longer be enough to safely cover the loan.

3

The position becomes risky

The protocol calculates a number called health factor. The lower it gets, the closer the position is to liquidation.

4

Liquidation can happen automatically

If the position becomes unsafe, the protocol allows liquidators to repay the debt and sell part of the collateral.

Explained simply

Key Concepts Without the Jargon.

A simple glossary for visitors who are not familiar with DeFi, blockchain or The Graph.

What is DeFi?

DeFi means decentralized finance. It refers to financial applications that run on blockchains instead of traditional banks.

What are Aave and Compound?

They are lending protocols. Users can deposit crypto, borrow crypto and earn or pay interest without a traditional intermediary.

What is collateral?

Collateral is the asset a borrower deposits to secure a loan. If the borrower becomes too risky, this collateral can be sold.

What is liquidation?

Liquidation happens when a borrowing position becomes unsafe. Part of the collateral is sold to repay the debt.

What is health factor?

Health factor is a risk number. If it gets close to 1, the position is close to liquidation. Higher usually means safer.

What is The Graph?

The Graph is a decentralized data network. It helps applications read blockchain data faster and more efficiently.

What is a subgraph?

A subgraph is like a custom API for blockchain data. It organizes raw blockchain events into information that apps can easily query.

What is GraphQL?

GraphQL is a query language. It lets developers ask for exactly the data they need, instead of downloading everything.

What is cascade risk?

Cascade risk appears when many positions can be liquidated together. One price drop can trigger forced selling, which may push prices lower.
